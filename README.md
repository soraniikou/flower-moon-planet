Tonight is the Flower Moon — May's full moon, 
named for the blossoms that bloom under its light.

I built this small app to mark the day.

This is a personal fan tribute, inspired by 
"Spinning Globe" (地球儀) — a song by Japanese 
artist Kenshi Yonezu, written for Studio Ghibli's 
The Boy and the Heron. It is not affiliated with, 
endorsed by, or officially connected to the artist 
or the studio.

—

On Earth Day this year, I did my usual chores. 
I went to work. My day was no different from 
any other day. I saved a little electricity, 
a little water — but nothing more.

The only thing I can really do to heal myself 
is listen to music, and sing.

So I made this small app — not to change the 
world, but to soften the way I meet my own 
ordinary day.

—

The app asks one quiet question:
Think of someone.

That's all.

For two minutes, you hold a name, a memory, 
or a face in your thoughts. A small planet 
turns gently in front of you. And then a 
message appears:

"The moment you thought of someone, even briefly, 
changes the world."

—

If, for two minutes on this Flower Moon night, 
you can hold someone in your thoughts, 
maybe that is enough.
